The GenAI economy just got its first real revenue number

It is bigger than most people officially admitted — and it is not coming from where the headlines pointed.

Tiernan Allen July 6, 2026 4 min read

For two years the generative-AI economy was measured in funding rounds and demos. This quarter it finally has a number tied to work actually delivered.

Where the revenue is

Not from chat toys, but from systems that complete real tasks end to end: support resolved, documents processed, pipelines kept clean.

The shift is subtle but decisive — buyers stopped paying for capability and started paying for outcomes.

What it means

Vendors that can point to completed work, not benchmark scores, are the ones capturing budget. Everyone else is discounting.
