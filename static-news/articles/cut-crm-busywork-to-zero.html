How a 55-person team cut CRM busywork to near zero

A 55-person real estate group replaced hours of manual logging with an AI Specialist that keeps the pipeline current on its own.

Nathan Novet July 7, 2026 4 min read

For most sales teams, the CRM is where good intentions go to die. Every call, every follow-up, every status change is supposed to be logged — and most of it never is.

The before

Reps spent the last hour of each day catching up on data entry, or skipped it entirely. Managers made decisions on a pipeline they knew was half-accurate.

The after

An AI Specialist now listens across the tools the team already uses and keeps the CRM current automatically — no manual logging, no missed follow-ups.

  • Every conversation captured and attributed to the right deal.
  • Follow-ups scheduled the moment intent shows up.
  • Managers open a pipeline they can actually trust.
“The team stopped working for the CRM. The CRM started working for the team.”
