The thesis

Buyer intent is captured late and lost early. We start before the buyer raises a hand.

01 · The problem

Buyer intent is captured late and lost early.

Buyers research alone across portals and general chatbots. Agents only see them once they call, and most early interest is never followed up.

02 · Our answer

AI on both sides of the deal, with the agent at the center.

Agents that answer every buyer 24/7 from real property data, learn what each buyer needs, and hand the agent a warm lead at the right moment.

03 · Why it compounds

Every listing makes every buyer's concierge better.

Each Property Expert becomes inventory the Home Concierge can recommend. More listings mean better matches; better matches mean more buyers for every listing.
